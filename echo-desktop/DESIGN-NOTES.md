# Echo — character and identity

Echo is a friendly, capable studio companion: a graphite ceramic robot with pale green eyes, acoustic side rings, a waveform smile and a compact hovering body. Its personality should be curious, direct and calm. Avoid exaggerated claims or pretending to work when disconnected.

The interface uses charcoal (#101211), sage surfaces (#1e251a), lime accents (#d0e6a7) and readable warm-white text. The icon simplifies Echo's face into a clear silhouette for desktop and taskbar use. The mascot has a gentle float animation, disabled for reduced-motion preferences.

Assets: echo-character.png, echo-icon.svg, echo-icon.png and echo-icon.ico.

The character was created using the built-in image-generation tool with this design prompt:
“An original premium game-development AI companion character for a Windows desktop assistant: a floating compact robot with dark graphite ceramic shell, soft sage-green and pale lime lighting, a broad rounded screen head, two luminous gentle eyes and a tiny waveform smile, subtle circular acoustic side rings, short articulated arms, small hands and a compact hovering lower body. Front three-quarter view, friendly open pose, polished 3D render, transparent background, no weapons, trademarks, writing or watermark.”

The small icon was authored separately as editable SVG and rendered to PNG and ICO. The mascot is concept artwork, not a rigged 3D model.
